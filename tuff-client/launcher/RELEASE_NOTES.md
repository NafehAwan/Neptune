Glade 1.1.0
- Glade's in-game mod is now in every Fabric 26.3 profile, kept up to date and checked before each launch. It can't be removed by accident.
- Before launching, Glade fixes common problems: missing or turned-off Fabric API, two copies of the same mod, and old Glade copies.
- Glade now updates itself (the launcher and the in-game mod).
- New in game: Armor HUD with durability colors, Hit Sounds, Item Physics, Old Animations, Low Fire, Small Shield, Hand & Item View, Time & Weather.

# Glade cosmetics

`users.json` lists the Minecraft UUIDs (with dashes) of Glade players. The Glade
mod downloads it from the `master` branch and shows a leaf badge before their
names (and their Glade cape, for players without a cape of their own). You
always see your own badge and cape.

Add someone by putting their UUID in the list:

```json
["069a79f4-44e9-4726-a5be-fca90e38aaf5"]
```
